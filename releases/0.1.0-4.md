# Sovel desktop 0.1.0-4

- Redesigned Store: Discover, Create, Work, Play and Develop collections, searchable Flatpak catalog, publisher icons/screenshots, app detail pages, permission review and Install/Open actions.
- Twenty real app listings are bundled for immediate browsing. Online details come from Flathub. Ratings and rankings are not fabricated.
- Flathub is initialized for the signed-in account when needed; installed apps appear in the Installed collection.
- Motion Wallpapers: a built-in aurora and silent looping local video backgrounds. Available from Store, Settings and App Library. Reduced Motion/Low Effects and maximized windows pause playback. Steam Workshop projects are not supported.
- Wi-Fi: network enumeration survives repeated property notifications; adapter scan failures and timeouts are visible instead of an indefinite scanning state.
- Desktop surfaces now follow screen addition/removal and geometry changes.

After installing this system update, restart Sovel to load the new desktop.
Physical scanning on the Foxtec AIC8800D80 adapter and the reported GTX 1070 full-system lockup remain unverified on real hardware.
